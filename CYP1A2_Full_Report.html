Conclusions

Key Findings

  • CYP1A2 genotype strongly predicts metabolizer status (χ²=86.69, p=6.65×10⁻¹⁸)
  • AA genotype → predominantly Intermediate metabolizers (80.6%)
  • AG genotype → mixed phenotype: Intermediate (57%) and Poor (43%)
  • GG genotype → exclusively Poor metabolizers (100%)
  • All pairwise comparisons statistically significant (Fisher p=1×10⁻⁴)
  • ML models confirm genotype as meaningful predictor (73.3% accuracy)
  • Cross-validation confirms model stability (CV mean=0.73, SD=0.04)
Clinical Implications
Individuals with GG genotype are at risk of drug accumulation with CYP1A2-metabolized medications. Pharmacogenomic screening may help personalize drug dosing in psychiatric and respiratory care.
